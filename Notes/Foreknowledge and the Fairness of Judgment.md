# Foreknowledge and the Fairness of Judgment

God can't fairly judge someone for an action he already foreknew would happen, especially if he does nothing to prevent it and then punishes them for it anyway. Knowing something will happen isn't the same as causing it, but foreknowledge plus non-intervention plus judgment is a fairness/justice problem, not just a free-will/causation problem.
