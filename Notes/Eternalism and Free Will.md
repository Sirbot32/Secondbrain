# Eternalism and Free Will

The future is already definite — like a fourth dimension that exists all at once, the way three-dimensional space exists all at once even though we only experience it by moving through it. We can't perceive or travel through the fourth dimension (time) the way we move through the other three. From inside it, as three-dimensional beings, actions feel free in the moment, even though the whole timeline is already fixed when viewed from outside it.
