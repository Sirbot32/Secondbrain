# Inflation (Monetary Causes)

One real driver of inflation: when more money is printed and put into circulation without a matching increase in goods/services, that extra money chases the same amount of stuff, pushing prices up. (Not the only cause — supply shocks and demand surges can also drive inflation — but this monetary mechanism is a genuine one.)
