# Utilitarianism vs Deontology

Utilitarianism judges an action by its outcome; deontology judges the act itself against a moral rule, regardless of outcome. Taking revenge on someone who murdered your family is immoral both as an act and in its outcome. But stealing from a company that's exploiting people, where the theft directly saves many lives, is an immoral act that produces a moral outcome — the outcome is the more important measure there.
