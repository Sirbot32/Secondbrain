# Irrational Numbers

A number that cannot be expressed as a fraction (ratio of two integers) — equivalently, its decimal expansion goes on forever without ever repeating. "Cannot be expressed as a fraction" alone is the complete definition; the infinite-decimal property is just a true consequence of it, not a separate test (e.g. 1/3 = 0.333... is infinite but repeating, and still rational, because it *can* be written as a fraction).
