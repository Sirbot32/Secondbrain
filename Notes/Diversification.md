# Diversification

Diversification spreads investment across different assets/sectors so that a downturn in one (e.g. tech having a bad day) doesn't sink the whole portfolio, since other holdings can offset the loss. Related principle: a genuinely zero-risk investment should carry zero excess reward — risk and expected reward are linked.
