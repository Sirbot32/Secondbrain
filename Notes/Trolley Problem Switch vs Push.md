# Trolley Problem Switch vs Push

Intuition flips between the two trolley variants. Pulling a switch to divert the trolley doesn't make you the executioner — you just happen to save the people on the main track. Pushing someone to stop the trolley makes you the executioner directly; the physical and causal distance between you and the death changes the moral weight, even though the outcome (one dies, several live) is the same either way.
