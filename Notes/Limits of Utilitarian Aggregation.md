# Limits of Utilitarian Aggregation

Pure utilitarian math can say it's worth torturing one innocent person if it makes enough other people slightly happier — but that's wrong. An average happiness number doesn't capture what's actually happening to the one person being tortured. There's a hard limit: don't torture a morally innocent person, regardless of how the math comes out.
