# Virus vs Bacteria

A virus isn't fully "alive" on its own — it needs a host cell's machinery to reproduce, and does so by hijacking and often damaging that host. A bacterium is a self-sufficient single-celled living organism that reproduces on its own (via binary fission) and can be harmless, helpful, or harmful depending on the species.
