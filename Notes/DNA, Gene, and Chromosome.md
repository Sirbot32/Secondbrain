# DNA, Gene, and Chromosome

DNA is the molecule that encodes proteins, built from base pairs that match up (G with C, A with T). A gene is a specific segment of DNA that can be expressed ("on") or not ("off") — e.g. the gene for growth hormone release turns off after puberty in most people. A chromosome is an entire packaged DNA strand; human body cells carry full sets of chromosomes, while sex cells (sperm/egg) carry half that number so they combine to a full set at fertilization.
