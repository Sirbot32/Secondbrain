# Calorie vs Nutrient

A calorie is a unit of energy/heat the body can extract from food. A nutrient is a substance the body needs to carry out specific biological processes (e.g. glucose for cellular respiration) — calories measure energy quantity, nutrients are about functional building blocks, not interchangeable concepts.
