# Dividend

A dividend is a cash payment a company makes to shareholders out of its profits, used as an incentive to hold or buy more of its stock. The per-share amount can be small (e.g. Nvidia pays a small fractional-dollar dividend per share) but compounds in value when held over a long time and across many shares.
