# Median Resistance to Outliers

The median only depends on a value's position in sorted order, not its magnitude, so one extreme outlier can't drag it — unlike the mean, which sums magnitude and gets pulled toward extreme values. E.g. with 5 ones, 5 twos, and one value of a trillion (11 points total), the 6th sorted value — the median — is still 2, completely unmoved by the trillion. The mean, by contrast, would spike enormously.
