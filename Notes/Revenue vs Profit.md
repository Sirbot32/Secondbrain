# Revenue vs Profit

Revenue is a company's total income from sales. Profit is what's left after subtracting costs (operating expenses, etc.) from revenue. Note: revenue itself is not the same thing as "gross profit" — gross profit is revenue minus cost of goods sold specifically, a distinct intermediate figure between revenue and net profit.
