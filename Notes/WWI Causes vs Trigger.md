# WWI Causes vs Trigger

WWI's underlying causes were the web of alliances and secret treaties that pulled many countries in once fighting started. The immediate trigger was the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand, which set off a chain reaction in the Balkans (Russia backing Serbia, Austria-Hungary backing by Germany) that escalated into full alliance-block war.

Note: "Yugoslavia" did not exist yet in 1914 — the relevant country was Serbia. Yugoslavia wasn't formed until after WWI.
