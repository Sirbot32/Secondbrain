# About Me — Goals & Learning Style

Captured from the 100 Questions survey (2026-06-23). Living doc — update as preferences change.

## Why I want this assistant
I want a local AI assistant that doesn't just follow trends, builds things to my taste, and that I can manage like a team working for me. This assistant (you) is the one I trust most to relay that to the others.

## Goals
- Become genuinely expert in: Machine Learning, Stock Pricing, and Electronics, over the next year.
- Long-term: a daily-use assistant that, during school, pulls from Canvas/Infinite Campus and surfaces what's due; on my phone, surfaces tasks ranked by priority.
- Summer goal: build something appealing to big finance firms / a strong LinkedIn presence, and make money without needing a degree to survive — while still getting a degree to actually learn, not just to get by.

## Learning style
- Time: aiming for 2+ hours Mon–Fri, but mostly driven by genuine interest in the moment.
- Prefer learning through direct experience/hands-on practice over reading or video.
- Want the technical/precise definition first, not the analogy — happy to revisit it.
- Like being quizzed/tested, but don't think testing alone is a great measure of real understanding vs. memorization.
- Night person; prefer longer, deep-focus sessions over short daily ones.
- When wrong: let me finish the thought first, then correct it and help me re-state it better — don't interrupt mid-thought.
- Fine with direct "you don't actually know this as well as you think you do" feedback.
- Explicitly wants real pushback if reasoning gets lazy or sloppy — "keep it real."

## Background
- Currently unemployed, a student — studying to expand knowledge generally, not tied to a specific job.
- Tried and gave up on learning to code before — didn't know where to start or what the process would look like.
- Successfully self-taught philosophy/religion through reading, reasoning, and debate — this is the model for what worked.
