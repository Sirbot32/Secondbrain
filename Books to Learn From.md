# Books to Learn From

Books mapped to specific gaps logged in [[Learning Queue]] — read these to close a gap, not just because the topic is interesting.

## AI
- *Make Your Own Neural Network* — Tariq Rashid. Builds a neural net from scratch in Python; directly covers weighted sum + bias, activation functions, and backpropagation/gradient descent.
- *Artificial Intelligence: A Very Short Introduction* — Margaret A. Boden. Covers the actual AI / machine learning / deep learning hierarchy.
- *Grokking Deep Learning* — Andrew Trask. Beginner-level, code-driven coverage of the same fundamentals (hidden layers, backprop), less math notation than Rashid's book.

## Philosophy
- *The Consolation of Philosophy* — Boethius. The original classical source for the "God outside time" solution to the foreknowledge problem.
- *Time: A Very Short Introduction* — Jenann Ismael. Covers eternalism / the block universe as a named view in philosophy of time.
- *Four Views on Free Will* — John Martin Fischer, Robert Kane, Derk Pereboom, Manuel Vargas. Direct, structured coverage of compatibilism alongside the other major positions.
- *Reasons and Persons* — Derek Parfit. Source of the aggregation/separateness-of-persons critique of utilitarianism.
- *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* — Immanuel Kant. Primary source for the means/ends formula relevant to the trolley push variant; also covers the deontological framework underlying the doctrine of double effect.
- *God, Freedom, and Evil* — Alvin Plantinga. Standard philosophy-of-religion text on theodicy and the free will defense against the problem of evil.
