# Books of Interest

Books matching topics I've shown genuine interest in during deep-dive sessions — not tied to specific knowledge gaps, just things worth reading because the subject pulled me in.

## AI
- *Superintelligence* — Nick Bostrom. Long-term AI risk and alignment, including the kind of deceptive/treacherous-turn scenarios discussed in [[Deceptive Alignment Concern]].
- *Human Compatible* — Stuart Russell. AI safety and alignment from a leading AI researcher's perspective.

## Philosophy
- *Free Will* — Sam Harris. Short, accessible argument against free will as commonly conceived.
- *Freedom Evolves* — Daniel Dennett. Compatibilist counterpoint to Harris — free will as compatible with a deterministic universe.
- *Would You Kill the Fat Man?* — David Edmonds. Accessible tour of the trolley problem and what it reveals about moral intuition.
- *Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do?* — Michael Sandel. Walkthrough of utilitarianism vs. deontology using real cases.
